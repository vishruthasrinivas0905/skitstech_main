WISER: What is Strategically and Essentially Required.

WISER is one of SKITS’ flagship AI-driven innovation-intelligence platforms, conceived as an end-to-end platform for idea capture, novelty assessment, global technology and patent searches, IP generation, product and market intelligence and commercialisation strategy.

It moves beyond conventional keyword-based searches toward semantic and contextual intelligence, connecting novelty assessment with IP strategy, compliance intelligence, product development and commercial planning.

WISER will accelerate research and IP generation, protection, commercialisation, and regulatory compliance of individuals, startups, educational institutions, enterprises, and PSUs.
