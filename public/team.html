Meet the team

CONSULTANT01

Dr S K Murthy

IP · R&D · ACADEMIA
Registered Indian Patent Agent

Brings 32 years of combined experience across intellectual property, R&D, and academia, with deep expertise in US, European, and Indian patent practices. As a registered Indian Patent Agent and certified Independent Director, they have a proven track record of building innovation cultures within multinational corporations and navigating complex data protection and corporate governance matters. Over their career, they have chaired a global IP committee overseeing more than 6,000 patent assets, drafted over 250 patent applications, and co-founded the In-House IP Professionals (IHIPP) Forum. Beyond corporate leadership, they have actively shaped India’s IP and Standards policies, serve on the Government of Karnataka’s ESDM vision group, and advise several engineering schools. Their extensive academic foundation includes a Ph.D. from NLSIU, a D.Sc. in Computer Science, an LL.B., and an M.E. (Gold Medallist), complemented by multiple published books and a commissioned paper for the National Academy of Sciences, USA.

CONSULTANT02

Ananth Sripadarao

IP LAW & LEGAL AFFAIRS
Patent & Trademark Agent · Advocate

Brings over 20 years of distinguished experience in intellectual property law and legal affairs across the APAC, Middle East, and Africa regions. With a strong foundation established through key roles at Philips India Ltd. and 3M India Ltd., he possesses deep expertise in patents, trademarks, brand protection, industrial designs, and corporate governance. Over his career, he has maintained a 100% success rate in leading major IP litigations and complex licensing negotiations, while also successfully filing more than 200 patent applications and commercializing over 300 technologies. A dual-registered Indian Patent and Trademark Agent and Karnataka State Bar advocate, he is also a Registered Independent Director, a recognized M&A expert, and a Certified Arbitrator with memberships in LES and CIArb, London. His robust strategic and technical background is supported by an LL.M. from the National University of Singapore (NUS), a B.Sc. in Chemistry, and specialized qualifications in Patent Law from NALSAR and Advanced Corporate Law from IIM Kolkata.
