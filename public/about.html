About Us

An AI and SaaS-based technology company developing intelligent digital platforms for research, innovation, intellectual property, technology intelligence, product strategy and commercialisation.

OUR STORY

Information into intelligence.

Satatham Kritam Innovative Technology Solutions Private Limited (SKITS) is a technology and innovation-intelligence company focused on developing Artificial Intelligence (AI), SaaS-based platforms, analytical tools and intelligent digital solutions for research, technology development, intellectual property, innovation management and commercialisation.

SKITS develops technology solutions that help innovators, researchers, universities, start-ups, enterprises and R&D organisations convert complex scientific, technical, patent and market information into meaningful and actionable intelligence. Its platforms support the complete innovation lifecycle, from idea evaluation, technology and prior-art intelligence, IP creation and protection, product-development strategy and market analysis through to licensing and commercialisation.

  • Idea to impact — From idea evaluation and technology and prior-art intelligence to IP creation and protection, product strategy, market analysis and commercialisation
  • AI & SaaS platforms — Secure, scalable, AI-enabled SaaS and on-premise solutions for organisations of different sizes
  • Sector-neutral — R&D, intellectual property, education, healthcare, semiconductors, quantum technologies, cybersecurity, aerospace and other technology-intensive fields
  • Built for the innovation ecosystem — Start-ups and MSMEs, universities and research institutions, enterprises, R&D organisations, incubators and IP professionals
VISION & MISSION

Transform ideas into value.

OUR VISION

To become a leading technology and innovation-intelligence company developing intelligent digital platforms that accelerate research, protect innovation, enable informed decision-making and transform ideas into commercial and societal value.

OUR MISSION

Transform information into intelligence, intelligence into innovation, and innovation into value.
