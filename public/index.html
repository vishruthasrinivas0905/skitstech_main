ABOUT US

Gives your mind a new way of thinking.

Satatham Kritam Innovative Technology Solutions Private Limited (SKITS) is a technology and innovation-intelligence company focused on developing Artificial Intelligence (AI), SaaS-based platforms, analytical tools and intelligent digital solutions for research, technology development, intellectual property, innovation management and commercialisation.

  • Idea to impact — From idea evaluation and technology and prior-art intelligence to IP creation and protection, product strategy, market analysis and commercialisation
  • AI & SaaS platforms — Secure, scalable, AI-enabled SaaS and on-premise solutions for organisations of different sizes
  • Sector-neutral — R&D, intellectual property, education, healthcare, semiconductors, quantum technologies, cybersecurity, aerospace and other technology-intensive fields
  • Built for the innovation ecosystem — Start-ups and MSMEs, universities and research institutions, enterprises, R&D organisations, incubators and IP professionals

Ask us directly ↗
What does SKITS help with?+

SKITS develops AI-enabled SaaS and on-premise platforms, analytical tools and knowledge systems that support research, technology intelligence, patent and IP analytics, product strategy and commercialisation, from idea evaluation through to licensing and market deployment.

When should I speak with an IP professional?+

Consider getting advice before publicly disclosing an invention, launching a brand, entering a commercial arrangement or making decisions that could affect ownership or protection of IP.

Do you work with startups and growing businesses?+

Yes. We support innovators and businesses at different stages, helping them shape practical legal and IP approaches around their goals and resources.

Can you support matters outside India?+

We work with trusted professionals across jurisdictions to coordinate international IP strategies and related legal support.

How do I get started?+

Send us a short note about your question using the contact form. We will respond to discuss next steps. Please do not include confidential or time-sensitive information in an initial enquiry.
