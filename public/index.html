Ask us directly ↗
What does SKITS help with?+

SKITS develops AI-enabled SaaS and on-premise platforms, analytical tools and knowledge systems that support research, technology intelligence, patent and IP analytics, product strategy and commercialisation, from idea evaluation through to licensing and market deployment.

When should I speak with an IP professional?+

Consider getting advice before publicly disclosing an invention, launching a brand, entering a commercial arrangement or making decisions that could affect ownership or protection of IP.

Do you work with startups and growing businesses?+

Yes. We support innovators and businesses at different stages, helping them shape practical IP and innovation approaches around their goals and resources.

Can you support matters outside India?+

We work with trusted professionals across jurisdictions to coordinate international IP strategies and related support.

How do I get started?+

Send us a short note about your question using the contact form. We will respond to discuss next steps. Please do not include confidential or time-sensitive information in an initial enquiry.
